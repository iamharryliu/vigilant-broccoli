## Kibble Toppers

### Proteins

| Food | Preparation | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken, turkey, or lean beef | Cooked, unseasoned, shredded, boneless | Lean protein |
| Egg | Boiled or scrambled, plain | Protein |
| Salmon | Cooked, boneless, not smoked | Omega-3 fatty acids |
| Sardines | In water, no added salt | Omega-3 fatty acids |

### Vegetables

| Food | Preparation | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broccoli | Lightly steamed or plain | Fiber |
| Carrots | Raw, grated, or steamed | Crunch and fiber |
| Cucumber | Chopped | Hydration, low calories |
| Green beans | Steamed or plain frozen | Low-calorie fiber |
| Pumpkin | Plain canned purée, not pie filling | Fiber and digestion |
| Sweet potato | Cooked, mashed | Fiber and carbohydrates |

### Fruits

| Food | Preparation | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple | Chopped, no core or seeds | Crunch and fiber |
| Banana | Sliced, in moderation | Sweet treat |
| Blueberries | Fresh or frozen, in moderation | Antioxidants |
| Watermelon | Seedless, no rind, in moderation | Hydration |

### Extras

| Food | Preparation | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bone broth | Low-sodium, drizzled | Flavor and hydration |
| Warm water | Pour directly onto kibble | Softens kibble, enhances aroma |
| Yogurt | Plain, unsweetened | Protein, calcium, and digestion |

## Avoid Feeding

- Onions, garlic
- Grapes/raisins
- Avocado
- Chocolate
- Macadamia nuts
- Xylitol
- Seasoned/salty foods
- Fatty scraps
