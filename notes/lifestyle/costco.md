Costco suits non-perishables, household essentials, frozen food and products used consistently; the biggest savings come from long shelf lives and low waste.

## Before Buying

- Compare unit prices: Costco isn't always cheaper than grocery-store sales.
- Check storage space: a chest freezer makes bulk meat and frozen food far more practical.
- Watch expiration dates: oils, nuts, flour and coffee lose freshness.
- Avoid bulk purchases of unfamiliar products: try smaller quantities first.
- Consider consumption rate: a 6-month supply of detergent makes sense; of fresh vegetables, it doesn't.
