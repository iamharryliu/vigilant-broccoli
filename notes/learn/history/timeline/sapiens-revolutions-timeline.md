## Revolutions

| Revolution | Approx. Date | What Changed | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cognitive Revolution | ~70,000 - 30,000 BCE | Sapiens gained flexible language and the ability to share fictions (myths, gods, tribes). | Large-scale cooperation among strangers; spread out of Africa; extinction of much megafauna. |
| Agricultural Revolution | ~10,000 BCE | Wheat, rice, sheep, cattle and other species domesticated; foragers settled in villages. | Population growth, property, hierarchy and harder work; the "biggest fraud in history". |
| Unification of Humankind | ~1,000 BCE onward | Money, empires and universal religions linked separate cultures into one system. | Trade networks, large empires and shared belief systems. |
| Scientific Revolution | ~1500 CE | Admitting ignorance, observation plus mathematics, and the aim to gain power through new technology. | Empire and science fund each other; Europe's overseas expansion. |
| Capitalist Revolution | ~1500 CE onward | Credit and trust in the future let the economy grow rather than stay a fixed pie; profit is reinvested. | Banks, joint-stock companies and global trade. |
| Industrial Revolution | ~1760 CE | Steam, then fossil fuels, converted energy into movement at scale; factories replaced the household. | Mass production, urbanization, family and community replaced by state and market. |
| Future Revolutions | 21st century | Biotechnology, cyborg engineering and non-organic AI may rewrite biology and intelligence. | Possible end of Homo sapiens as we know it. |

## Key Ideas

- **Imagined orders**: money, nations, corporations and human rights exist because enough people believe in them.
- **Three universal orders**: money (economic), empires (political) and universal religions (spiritual) unified the world.
- **Energy link**: the Industrial Revolution is an energy revolution; see [Energy Cycle and Conversion](../../../maths-and-sciences/physics/energy-cycle.md).
