# Online Meeting Prep

- Open the meeting window (Zoom/Meet/Teams) a few minutes early and join the call.
- Put on and test headphones/headset — check mic input and speaker output.
- Check camera framing, lighting, and background (blur or tidy up if on video).
- Mute mic by default; unmute only to speak.
- Close distracting tabs/apps and silence phone/desktop notifications.
- Plug in the charger — don't start a call on low battery.
- Check internet connection; run a speed test if it's been flaky.
- Have the agenda, notes doc, or relevant links open and ready.
- If presenting, open and pre-load the exact window/tab you'll screen share — close anything sensitive first.
- Keep water nearby.
- Do a quick bio break before the call starts.
