# Business Lingo

| Term  | Definition                                                                                                                           |
| :---- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ARR   | Annual Recurring Revenue — the yearly value of a company's recurring subscription revenue, normalized regardless of contract length. |
| B2B   | Business-to-Business — a company sells its product or service to other businesses rather than individual consumers.                  |
| B2C   | Business-to-Consumer — a company sells its product or service directly to individual consumers.                                      |
| CAC   | Customer Acquisition Cost — the total sales and marketing cost to acquire one new customer.                                          |
| Churn | The rate at which customers cancel or stop paying for a subscription over a given period.                                            |
| LTV   | Lifetime Value — the total revenue a business expects to earn from a customer over the life of the relationship.                     |
| MRR   | Monthly Recurring Revenue — the monthly value of a company's recurring subscription revenue.                                         |
| SaaS  | Software as a Service — software licensed on a subscription basis and hosted centrally, accessed by users over the internet.         |
