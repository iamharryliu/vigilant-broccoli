## Social Engagement

| Activity | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| Code with me sessions | Someone works through a real task while others join to watch, ask questions and pair; a low-pressure way to share how people work and learn from each other. |
| Tech chats | Informal talks where someone presents a technology, tool or lesson learned, followed by open discussion. |
| Weekly internal AMA | Interview someone, and that person interviews the next. A nice, easy thing to do once a week over lunch to get to know coworkers and people you are not familiar with. |
