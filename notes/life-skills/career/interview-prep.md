## Ask Yourself

| Factor | Category | Ask Yourself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product quality | Delivery | Is the product reliable, useful, and something you would be proud to work on? |
| Project opportunity | Delivery | Is the project greenfield or brownfield? Will you have opportunities to make architectural decisions? |
| Autonomy | Management | Will you be trusted to make decisions and choose how to approach your work without micromanagement? |
| Management | Management | Does management set clear expectations, provide support, and communicate openly? |
| Work arrangement | Management | Does the role offer flexible hybrid or remote work? |
| Work-life balance | Management | Are the working hours, workload, and expectations compatible with the life you want? |
| Team | People | Is the team collaborative and supportive, with people you can learn from and work well with? |
| Company security | Stability | Is the company financially stable, growing sustainably, and well positioned for the future? |
| Role security | Stability | How secure is the role given the company's financial health, priorities, and potential restructuring? |

## What I Care About

- Delivery and feeling my work matters.
  - Having ownership over my deliverables, from decisions through to shipping and follow-up.
- Opportunities to develop in my career, and it does not just have to be tech-wise.
- Autonomy and Ownership or Strong Leadership and being a nail to a hammer
  - Autonomy and ownership
    - Having control over how you approach your work.
    - Being trusted to make decisions without micromanagement.
    - Feeling a sense of responsibility and accountability for outcomes.
  - Being a nail to a hammer.
    - Having what to do outlined and I am able to execute without having to worry about unnecessary details.
      - Trust and communication.
- Management
  - Clear expectations
  - Reasonable work hours
- **Stability and Security**
  - Confidence in the company's future.
  - Transparent leadership.
  - Well structured workspace with clear processes

## Tell Me About Yourself

| Theme | What to Present | Talking Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collaboration | You work well with engineers, designers and stakeholders. | Pairing, code reviews, clear communication and early alignment. |
| Culture | You build community and share knowledge. | Mentoring, onboarding newcomers, documenting, demos and internal talks. |
| Delivery | You take work from idea to production and keep it running. | Shipping end to end, owning deployment and operating what you build. |
| Growth | You learn quickly and keep widening your range. | Picking up new tools, working across the stack and acting on feedback. |
| Impact | Your work saves time and moves a business metric. | Automating manual work, improving performance and tying work to outcomes. |
| Ownership | You take responsibility for outcomes, not only tasks. | Driving requirements, making decisions and following through after release. |
| Reliability | You build things that stay up and can be recovered. | Monitoring, alerting, backups, testing and calm incident handling. |

## Questions

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Opening](#opening-questions) | Why the role exists, to start the conversation. |
| [Product](#product-questions) | The product, its tech stack, engineering practices and debt. |
| [Team](#team-questions) | Who you work with, how they operate and onboarding. |
| [Performance](#performance-questions) | What success looks like and how it is measured. |
| [Culture](#culture-questions) | How the company works day to day and how people are supported. |
| [Flexibility](#flexibility-questions) | Remote, hybrid, office days and schedule flexibility. |
| [Stability](#stability-questions) | Company risks and long-term outlook. |
| [Interview End](#interview-end-questions) | Closing the interview and learning what happens next. |
| [Compensation](#compensation-questions) | Pay, perks and tools once an offer is likely. |

### Goals of Questions

| Goal | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Understand Logistics | How the hiring process runs, when it ends and where and when you would work. |
| Understand Compensation | What the role pays and what perks and tools come with it. |
| Understand Expectations / Responsibilities | What the role involves day to day and what success looks like. |
| Understand Environment / Management and Team | Who you would work with and for, how they work together and how people are supported. |
| Understand Practices | How engineering gets done: stack, testing, deployments, code quality and tech debt. |
| Understand Company Direction | Where the company is heading, its risks and whether it will be around and growing. |
| Understand Reflection | How the interviewer sees your fit and whether any concerns can be addressed before you leave. |

### Opening Questions

| Goal | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Logistics | • What does the hiring process look like?<br>• What is the expected hiring date? |
| Compensation | • What is the salary range? |
| Expectations / Responsibilities | • What are the expectations for the role? |
| Environment / Management and Team | • Why is role open? Are we replacing someone, or is it a new role on the team?<br>• What does the company value? |

### Product Questions

| Goal | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Environment / Management and Team | • What do they like about it.<br>• What do they dislike about it.<br>• What's the most interesting thing that they have worked on or learned on the project? |
| Practices | • Current stack and why it was chosen?<br>• What is the CI/CD approach?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ What does the code review process look like?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ What does the testing process look like?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ How are environments and deployments handled?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ How are scalability and performance handled?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ What are the documentation processes?<br>• Every company is carrying some amount of _technical debt_, what's the tech debt situation here?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ How much they think there is?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ What they're doing about it? |

### Team Questions

| Goal | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Expectations / Responsibilities | • What would the day to day look like for my role? What does it look like for you?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ How much time do you have available to develop versus being in meetings?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ Which meetings are mandatory, such as team syncs, one-on-ones with the manager or all-hands, and how often?<br>• What's the balance between firefighting and project work in an average week?<br>• How much ownership do I have over technical decisions? |
| Environment / Management and Team | • What is the team structure and what are the working practices?<br>• What are the biggest challenges that the team is facing?<br>• Who would be the direct manager?<br>• How is the onboarding process for a new hire? |
| Company Direction | • How does leadership prioritize long-term technical investments vs. short-term business needs?<br>• Where do they see the team in a year? |
