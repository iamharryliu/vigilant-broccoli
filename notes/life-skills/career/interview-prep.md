## Ask Yourself

| Factor              | Category   | Ask Yourself                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product quality     | Delivery   | Is the product reliable, useful, and something you would be proud to work on?                         |
| Project opportunity | Delivery   | Is the project greenfield or brownfield? Will you have opportunities to make architectural decisions? |
| Autonomy            | Management | Will you be trusted to make decisions and choose how to approach your work without micromanagement?   |
| Management          | Management | Does management set clear expectations, provide support, and communicate openly?                      |
| Work arrangement    | Management | Does the role offer flexible hybrid or remote work?                                                   |
| Work-life balance   | Management | Are the working hours, workload, and expectations compatible with the life you want?                  |
| Team                | People     | Is the team collaborative and supportive, with people you can learn from and work well with?          |
| Company security    | Stability  | Is the company financially stable, growing sustainably, and well positioned for the future?           |
| Role security       | Stability  | How secure is the role given the company's financial health, priorities, and potential restructuring? |

## What I Care About

- Delivery and feeling my work matters.
- Opportunities to develop in my career, and it does not just have to be tech-wise.
- Autonomy and Ownership or Strong Leadership and being a nail to a hammer
  - Autonomy and ownership
    - Having control over how you approach your work.
    - Being trusted to make decisions without micromanagement.
    - Feeling a sense of responsibility and accountability for outcomes.
  - Being a nail to a hammer.
    - Having what to do outlined and I am able to execute without having to worry about unnecessary details.
      - Trust and communication.
- Management
  - Clear expectations
  - Reasonable work hours
- **Stability and Security**
  - Confidence in the company's future.
  - Transparent leadership.
  - Well structured workspace with clear processes

## Questions

### Exit Interview Questions

- Sustainability
  - What does the day to day look like for you
  - What challenges are the teams facing
  - Why is role open?
- What are the next steps?

### Technical Questions

- How does the team handle code quality, testing, deployments and CI/CD?
- Current stack and why it was chosen?
- What's the balance between firefighting and project work in an average week?
- Every company is carrying some amount of _technical debt_, what's the tech debt situation here?
  - How much they think there is?
  - What they're doing about it?
- How does leadership prioritize long-term technical investments vs. short-term business needs?

### Culture and Team Questions

- How would you describe the culture and how is it maintained if there is one?
- How does the company support professional development?
- Is there an active mentoring strategy?
- What are the teams like?
- How much time do you have available to develop versus being in meetings?
- What do you like most about working here?
- What do you wish you could improve?
- About the product:
  - What do they like about it.
  - What do they dislike about it.
  - What's the most interesting thing that they have worked on or learned on the project?

### Further Stage Questions

- Probing
  - Where do they see the team in a year?
- Team
  - Who would be the direct manager?
  - How is the onboarding process for a new hire?
- Performance
  - What does being successful look like in the first 90 days
  - How is performance measured and how often is feedback given?

### Stability Questions

- What are the biggest risks or unknowns the company is facing in the near future?
- Where do you see the company in 3-5 years?

### Negotiation Questions

- Salary expectations
- What amenities are there?
  - coding assistant provider?
  - phone provider?
  - cloud services ie Google Workspace?

## Office Policy Types

| Policy          | Meaning                                                         | What to Ask                                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed hybrid    | Set office days each week, usually the same for the whole team. | Which days, and can they change?                                 |
| Flexible hybrid | A minimum number of office days, chosen by you or the team.     | What is the minimum, and is it tracked?                          |
| Fully on-site   | All work happens at the office.                                 | Is occasional remote work allowed, for example when sick?        |
| Fully remote    | No office is required; work happens anywhere.                   | Are there location, time zone, or in-person meetup requirements? |
| Office-first    | The office is the default and remote work needs a reason.       | How often is remote approved in practice?                        |
| Remote-first    | Remote is the default and the office is optional or occasional. | Are decisions and meetings documented for remote staff?          |

## Employment and Work Terms

| Term                | Meaning                                                             | What to Ask                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compensatory time   | Time off given in place of paid overtime.                           | How is it requested, and does it expire?                   |
| Consultant          | Hired out to clients through a consultancy, often on assignments.   | How are assignments chosen, and what happens between them? |
| Fixed-term contract | Employment that ends on a set date unless renewed.                  | What is the end date, and how often is it converted?       |
| Flextime            | Flexible start and end times around core hours.                     | What are the core hours?                                   |
| Full-time           | The standard working week, set by the contract or country.          | How many hours per week, including breaks?                 |
| Notice period       | Time you or the employer must give before employment ends.          | How long is it for each side?                              |
| On-call             | Being reachable outside working hours to handle incidents.          | How often is the rotation, and is it paid or compensated?  |
| Overtime            | Work beyond contracted hours.                                       | Is it expected, paid, or given back as time off?           |
| Part-time           | Fewer hours than full-time, with pay and some benefits scaled down. | Which benefits scale, and can hours change later?          |
| Permanent contract  | Employment with no end date.                                        | Is there a probation period first?                         |
| Probation period    | An initial period when either side can end employment more easily.  | How long is it, and what notice applies during it?         |
