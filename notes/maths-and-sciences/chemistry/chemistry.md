# Combustible Gases

| Gas       | Formula                  | Common Source/Use                                            | Notes                                                                                          |
| --------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Acetylene | C₂H₂                     | Oxy-fuel welding and cutting torches.                        | Widest flammable range of any common gas (~2.5%–100% in air); unstable above ~15 psi.          |
| Butane    | C₄H₁₀                    | Lighters, portable camping stoves, aerosol propellant.       | Heavier than air, so it pools in low spots when leaked.                                        |
| Ethane    | C₂H₆                     | Component of natural gas; feedstock for ethylene/plastics.   | Odorless — natural gas is odorized separately with mercaptan for leak detection.               |
| Hydrogen  | H₂                       | Fuel cells, rocket fuel, industrial processes.               | Burns with a nearly invisible flame; lightest gas, so it disperses upward quickly when leaked. |
| Methane   | CH₄                      | Main component of natural gas; produced by decomposition.    | Lighter than air; a potent greenhouse gas when unburned/vented.                                |
| Propane   | C₃H₈                     | Gas grills, home heating, forklifts (LPG).                   | Heavier than air; stored as a liquid under moderate pressure.                                  |
| Syngas    | Mixture (mainly H₂ + CO) | Industrial fuel gas produced from coal/biomass gasification. | Also toxic due to carbon monoxide content, not just flammable.                                 |
