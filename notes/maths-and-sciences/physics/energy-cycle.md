## Principle

- Energy is never created or destroyed, only converted between forms; each conversion loses some as waste heat.
- Almost all usable energy on Earth traces back to the Sun.

## Biological Energy Chain

Sun → plants → animals

- Sun: nuclear fusion releases light (radiant energy).
- Plants: photosynthesis converts light, water and CO₂ into sugars (chemical energy) and oxygen.
- Herbivores: eat plants and release the stored energy through respiration.
- Carnivores: eat herbivores; each step up passes on roughly 10% of the energy.
- Decomposers: return dead matter's nutrients and heat to the environment.

## Fossil Fuel Chain

Sun → plants → coal → steam → movement

- Ancient plants stored sunlight as chemical energy; buried and compressed over millions of years they became coal (animal remains and plankton became oil and gas).
- Burning coal converts chemical energy into heat.
- Heat boils water into high-pressure steam (thermal energy).
- Steam pushes a piston or turbine, giving mechanical energy (movement).
- A generator can then turn that movement into electricity.

## Conversion Steps

| Step | From → To | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combustion | Chemical → thermal | Burning coal in a boiler |
| Fusion | Nuclear → radiant | The Sun |
| Generation | Mechanical → electrical | Turbine driving a generator |
| Photosynthesis | Radiant → chemical | Leaf making sugar |
| Respiration | Chemical → mechanical + heat | Animal moving its muscles |
| Steam expansion | Thermal → mechanical | Steam engine piston |
