# Material Science Terms

| Term             | Description                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tarmac           | Short for `tarmacadam` — crushed stone/aggregate bound with tar, laid and compacted in layers to form a road surface. Patented by Edgar Purdue Hooley in 1901. |
| Macadam          | John Loudon McAdam's earlier method of layered, compacted crushed stone road-building, without a tar binder.                                                   |
| Asphalt Concrete | Aggregate bound with bitumen instead of tar; what most modern roads and airport aprons actually use. Often loosely called "tarmac" today.                      |
| Tar              | Coal-derived binder used in the original tarmacadam process.                                                                                                   |
| Bitumen          | Petroleum-derived binder that replaced tar through the 20th century — cheaper and less carcinogenic to work with.                                              |
