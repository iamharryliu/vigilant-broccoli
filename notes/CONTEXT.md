## Content Scope

- Keep notes project-agnostic: no repo apps, paths or hostnames.
- Prefer existing local notes. Add external links only when requested; they may appear inline with internal index links.

## Links and Reachability

Internal links must include `.md`. Every note must be reachable from an index. When adding or restructuring notes, check relative links and reachability; there is no automated check.

## Table of Contents Convention

Place a `## Table of Contents` directly under the title, with nested links to `##`/`###` headings. Add missing ToCs and rebuild them when headings change. Notes with no headings below the title are exempt.

## Topic-Specific Conventions

Read the applicable pattern before restructuring; topic-specific rules do not carry over to other topics.
