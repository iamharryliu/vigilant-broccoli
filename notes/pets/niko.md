# Niko 🐶

## Behaviour

- Usually sleeps during the midday time, usually likes to sleep under the sofa
- Nighttime he is a light sleeper and usually moves between different spots that he likes during the night.
- Scared/dislikes kids.

## Feeding

- Dry food/kibble - Pure Sensitive Turkey and Rice, Fill up plastic Mera cup and pour about half 2x a day. Niko asks for more during the day if he is hungry.
  - [Zoo](https://www.zoo.se/hund/produkter/pure-sensitive-adult-kalkon-ris?p=230923003)
  - [mera](https://merapetfood.se/products/mera-pure-sensitive-adult-kalkon-ris)
- We usually mix with some wet food if he does not seem interested in the dry food.
- Other foods he **loves**!
  - Boiled eggs
  - Cheese
  - Carrots
  - Dried fishes

## Walks

- Fine with going out 2x a day, ie morning and evening.
- He will want to go out but once he knows you are trying to put the harness on him or to grab him to go out he will run. Strategy is usually to trap him in a corner of pick him up before he knows that you are intending to take him out.
- Loves dogs and some humans, picky with who he lets touch him.
- Scared/dislikes kids avoid hälsa preferably.
- Usually has paws and ween wiped when returning home.

## What to watch out for

- Having his front paws touched. Usually tries to nip at our hands when we are wiping his front paws.
- He does not like walking over metal grates and avoids them like fire.
- His zoomies usually activates in open grass areas and will run around you in circles until he tires out.
