## Aperture

| F-number | Opening | Light | Depth of field | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| f/1.4 | Widest | Most | Shallowest | Low light, portraits |
| f/2.8 | Wide | High | Shallow | Street, low light, portraits |
| f/5.6 | Medium | Mid | Moderate | General shooting |
| f/8 | Narrow | Low | Deep | Landscapes, sharpest lens range |
| f/16 | Tiny | Least | Deepest | Landscapes, starbursts |

## Shutter Speed

| Shutter speed | Light | Motion | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2000 s | Least | Freezes fast action | Sports, birds, skating |
| 1/500 s | Low | Freezes moderate action | Kids, pets, walking |
| 1/125 s | Mid | Stops most subject blur | General handheld |
| 1/30 s | High | Blurs motion, needs steady hands | Low light, panning |
| 1 s | Most | Smooth water, light trails | Tripod night and long-exposure shots |
