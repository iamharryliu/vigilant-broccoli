## Goal

Brew the highest scoring potion by playing ingredient cards in front of you, paying for them with double-sided Sun/Moon runes.

## Limitations

| Limit            | Value                              | Notes                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Actions per turn | 2 per turn                         | May be the same action twice                                                 |
| Hand size        | 3 cards                            | Checked at the end of your turn, not during it                               |
| Market           | 4 day cards + 4 night cards        | Face up; refilled as cards are taken                                         |
| Players          | 2–4                                | Roughly 25 minutes                                                           |
| Rune slots       | 4, then 5, then 6                  | Start with 4; the 4th card played unlocks the 5th slot, the 8th card the 6th |
| Salt cards       | Must be played onto a card you own | Once placed it stays there                                                   |
| Sickles          | 2 per player                       | Single use each; spending one is free and does not cost an action            |
| Tableau          | 11 cards triggers the end          | The 6th and 10th cards each grant a bonus ingredient token                   |

## Setup

- Each player takes a player board, 1 random starting card placed in their tableau, 4 runes (2 Sun, 2 Moon), and 2 sickles.
- Reveal 4 cards from the Day Ingredient deck and 4 from the Night Ingredient deck as the market.
- Place the rune reserve and the bonus ingredient tokens in the centre.

## On Your Turn

Take 2 actions, in any order, the same or different:

1. Take a card
2. Play a card
3. Take runes
4. Flip runes

### 1. Take a Card

- Take 1 of the 8 face-up market cards into your hand.
- Refill the market from the matching deck.
- You may hold at most 3 cards at the end of your turn.

### 2. Play a Card

- Pay the rune cost printed on the card: a swoosh arrow flips that rune to its other side, an X discards it to the reserve.
- Place the card in your tableau, grouped with its ingredient type.
- Advance your tableau count by 1.

### 3. Take Runes

- Take runes of a single type from the reserve, up to your current rune slot capacity.

### 4. Flip Runes

- Flip runes on your board so that every face-up rune shows the same type, either all Suns or all Moons.

### Free Action: Sickle

- Discard 1 sickle to refresh either all 4 day cards or all 4 night cards.
- This is free and can be done on top of your 2 actions.

## Runes

- Runes are double sided: Sun on one face, Moon on the other.
- Costs either flip runes or return them to the reserve, so a cheap-looking card can still drain your board.
- Flipping keeps the rune; discarding does not.

## Tableau Track

| Card played | Unlock                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------ |
| 4th         | 1 extra rune slot        |
| 6th         | 1 bonus ingredient token |
| 8th         | 1 extra rune slot        |
| 10th        | 1 bonus ingredient token |

## Salt Cards

- Salt is a wild ingredient.
- It must be played onto a card already in your tableau, and stays there once placed.

## Game End

- The end begins when 1 player places their 11th card.
- Finish the current round so everyone has had an equal number of turns, then play 1 final round.

## Scoring

- Score every card in your potion, ingredient by ingredient.
- Cards score fixed points, points per matching ingredient, or points for combinations.
- The same cards and icons can count toward multiple scoring conditions.
- Highest total wins.

## Quick Tips

- Reaching the 4th and 8th cards early widens your rune capacity for the rest of the game.
- Costs that flip runes are far cheaper than costs that discard them; chain flips where you can.
- The Flip Runes action resets a mixed board, so plan pairs of cards that want the same face.
- Sickles are free, so spend them rather than ending the game holding them.
