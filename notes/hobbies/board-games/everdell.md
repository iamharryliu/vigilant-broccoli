## Goal

Build the highest scoring city of 15 cards over 4 seasons, using workers to gather twigs, resin, pebbles, and berries.

## Limitations

| Limit                     | Value                          | Notes                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| City size                 | 15 cards                       | Constructions and critters combined; cards like Wanderer state they take no city space |
| Forest locations revealed | 3 (2 players), 4 (3–4 players) | Each holds 1 worker with 2 players, 2 workers with 3–4 players                         |
| Hand size                 | 8 cards                        | Excess drawn cards are discarded immediately                                           |
| Journey entry             | City must hold 15 cards        | Autumn only                                                                            |
| Meadow                    | 8 face-up cards                | Refilled immediately whenever a card is taken                                          |
| Resources                 | Unlimited                      | Twigs, resin, pebbles, berries have no personal cap                                    |
| Special events            | 4 per game                     | Drawn from the event deck during setup                                                 |
| Unique cards              | 1 copy per city                | Common cards may be duplicated in the same city                                        |
| Workers                   | 6 by Autumn                    | Start 2, +1 in Spring, +1 in Summer, +2 in Autumn                                      |

## Setup

- Shuffle the 128 cards, deal 5 to each player, reveal 8 to the Meadow.
- Reveal the forest locations for the player count.
- Place the 4 basic events and reveal 4 special events.
- Each player starts with 2 workers in Winter and 1 extra resource depending on turn order.

## On Your Turn

Do exactly 1 action:

1. Place a worker
2. Play a card
3. Prepare for the next season

### 1. Place a Worker

- Basic and forest locations give resources or cards.
- Exclusive locations hold a single worker; unmarked locations hold any number.
- Red destination cards in any city can be visited; visiting another player's card pays them a benefit.

### 2. Play a Card

- Pay the card's resource cost, or play it for free through the construction that unlocks it.
- Cards go into your city and stay until game end.
- A city at 15 cards cannot take more cards.

### 3. Prepare for the Next Season

- Retrieve all workers, gain new workers, then take the season bonus.
- Spring: +1 worker, activate green production cards.
- Summer: +1 worker, draw up to 2 cards from the Meadow.
- Autumn: +2 workers, activate green production cards.

## Card Types

| Type        | Color  | Effect                                             |
| ----------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Destination | Red    | Provides a worker space, usable by any player      |
| Governance  | Blue   | Triggers when other cards are played               |
| Production  | Green  | Produces when played and at each season activation |
| Prosperity  | Purple | Scores bonus points at game end                    |
| Traveler    | Tan    | One-time effect when played                        |

## Events

- Basic events: claim by having 3 cards of a given color in your city, worth 3 points each.
- Special events: claim by meeting the named card requirements, worth more points.
- Claiming an event costs a worker placement.

## Journey

- Available in Autumn only, and only with a full 15-card city.
- Discard cards from hand equal to the space's value to score that value.
- Spaces are worth 5, 4, 3, and 2 points; the 2-point space fits any number of workers.

## Scoring

- Points printed on city cards.
- Points from claimed events and journey spaces.
- Point tokens collected during the game.
- Bonuses from purple prosperity cards.

## Quick Tips

- Free-play chains (construction unlocking its critter) stretch limited resources further.
- Green production cards played early pay out at every later season activation.
- Filling the city to 15 cards unlocks the journey, so hoarding cards late in Autumn is worth points.
- Watch the 8-card hand limit before taking card-heavy locations.
