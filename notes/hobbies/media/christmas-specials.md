# Christmas Specials

Holiday-themed special episodes from various anime series.

| Title                       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arnold Christmas special    | Arnold takes part in a secret Santa-style charity drive and is assigned to get a gift for his elderly boarding-house neighbor, Mr. Hyunh. Arnold learns that what Mr. Hyunh really wants isn't a material gift but to be reunited with the daughter he was separated from years earlier in Vietnam. |
| Initial D Christmas special | From the _Initial D: Third Stage_ movie. On a snowy Christmas/winter night, Takumi races down the mountain pass to help Natsuki get away from her ex, resolving their relationship arc.                                                                                                             |
