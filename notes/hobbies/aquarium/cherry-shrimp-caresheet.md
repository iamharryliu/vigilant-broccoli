# Cherry Shrimp Caresheet

## Water Parameters

- Temperature: 65-80°F (18-27°C), ideally 72-76°F.
- pH: 6.5-8.
- GH: 6-8 dGH.
- KH: 1-4 dKH.
- TDS: 150-250 ppm.
- Ammonia/Nitrite: 0 ppm — shrimp are very sensitive to spikes.
- Nitrate: under 20 ppm.

## Tank Setup

- Minimum tank size: 5 gallons, but larger volumes buffer parameter swings better.
- Fully cycle the tank before adding shrimp — they cannot tolerate an uncycled nitrogen cycle.
- Use a sponge filter — shrimp and shrimplets can get pulled into standard intake filters.
- Provide dense plants, moss (e.g. Java moss), and leaf litter (Indian almond/catappa leaves) for grazing surfaces and cover during molting.
- Substrate: inert or active (shrimp-specific buffering) substrate, avoid sharp gravel.

## Feeding

- Feed algae wafers, blanched vegetables (zucchini, spinach), or shrimp-specific pellets every 1-2 days.
- Remove uneaten food after a few hours to avoid fouling the water.
- Established tanks with biofilm and algae need little supplemental feeding.

## Molting

- Shrimp molt regularly as they grow; a shed exoskeleton is normal, leave it in the tank as it's a source of calcium.
- Provide calcium supplementation (cuttlebone or mineral balls) to support healthy molts.
- Shrimp are vulnerable and hide right after molting — avoid disturbing the tank during this time.

## Breeding

- Cherry shrimp breed readily in stable, mature tanks with no special intervention needed.
- Berried (egg-carrying) females carry eggs under their abdomen for 2-4 weeks before releasing free-swimming shrimplets.
- Keep copper-based medications out of the tank — copper is lethal to shrimp even in trace amounts.

## Water Changes

- Change 10-20% of water weekly, matching temperature and parameters closely to avoid shocking the colony.
- Drip acclimate new shrimp over 1-2 hours when introducing them to the tank.
