## Base

| Ingredient | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Carrot | Sweetness, color |
| Celery | Vegetal backbone, mild bitterness |
| Garlic | Savory depth |
| Napa Cabbage | Mild sweetness, light cabbage flavor, and umami from the thicker white stems; gives a clean, light rather than strongly aromatic stock |
| Onion | Sweetness, body |

- Napa + shiitake + scallion + ginger is particularly good for ramen and other Asian dishes.
- Keep napa in proportion to the other vegetables or it dominates the broth.

## Aromatics

| Ingredient | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Bay Leaf | Herbaceous, savory |
| Black Peppercorns | Warm, peppery background |
| Dried Shiitake | Major umami boost |
| Ginger | Bright, warming; pairs with napa |
| Scallion | Fresh onion character |

## Optional

| Ingredient | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Kombu | Umami; especially good for Asian-style stock |
| Smoked Paprika | Smoky depth, but pushes the stock toward a Western flavor |
| Truffle Oil | Finishing aroma; add off the heat |

## Other Additions

| Ingredient | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Parsley Stems | Fresh herbal flavor |
| Rosemary | Strong; use sparingly |
| Thyme | Classic Western stock flavor |

## Vegetable Scraps

| Ingredient | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Carrot Peels and Ends | Sweetness, color |
| Celery Leaves and Butt | Vegetal backbone, mild bitterness |
| Corn Cobs | Milky sweetness and a little starchy body |
| Garlic Skins and Nubs | Savory depth without the harshness of cloves |
| Ginger Peels | Bright, warming |
| Herb Stems | Fresh herbal flavor; parsley and cilantro |
| Leek and Scallion Tops | Fresh onion character |
| Mushroom Stems | Major umami boost, especially shiitake |
| Onion Skins and Ends | Sweetness, body, and a deep brown color |
| Tomato Cores | Acidity and umami |

## Instructions

- Cook down the vegetables, then simmer in water for 30-45 minutes.

## Notes

- Keep the stock relatively unsalted so it can be seasoned per dish later.
- Whole spices beat ground spices — use whole peppercorns, and go light on paprika.
- Leftover vegetables work as the base; the tables above are a starting point rather than a fixed recipe.
- For a seasoned, reusable braising liquid rather than a neutral stock, see [master stock](./master-stock.md).
