# Basil Care

Basil is a tender annual: it grows fast in warm weather and dies at the first frost. See [Basil](./basil.md) for cooking.

## Conditions

| Factor | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Container | Pot with drainage holes, at least 20 cm (8 in) wide |
| Light | 6-8 hours of direct sun; a grow light indoors in winter |
| Soil | Light, well-draining potting mix with some compost |
| Temperature | 20-30°C (68-86°F); stunted below 10°C (50°F) |

## Watering

- Water when the top 2 cm of soil is dry, at the base rather than over the leaves.
- Keep soil evenly moist but never soggy; wet roots rot quickly.
- Water in the morning so leaves dry before night.

## Harvesting

- Pinch stems just above a pair of side shoots so the plant branches instead of growing tall.
- Take no more than a third of the plant at once.
- Pinch off flower buds as soon as they appear; flowering turns leaves bitter and ends the plant's growth.
- Use cuttings in water to grow new plants: roots form in 1-2 weeks.

## Problems

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black spots or patches | Cold, or fungal disease from wet leaves | Keep above 10°C, water at the base, remove affected leaves |
| Holes in leaves | Slugs, snails or caterpillars | Pick them off by hand |
| Leggy, sparse growth | Too little light or no pinching | Move to more sun and pinch back |
| Yellow lower leaves | Overwatering or poor drainage | Let soil dry out and check drainage |
| Wilting despite moist soil | Root rot | Repot in fresh mix and trim rotted roots |
