# Rice

| Rice               | Grain Length | Origin         | Texture When Cooked     | Common Uses              | Notes                                                    |
| ------------------ | ------------ | -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arborio            | Short        | Italy          | Creamy, chewy           | Risotto                  | High surface starch, releases creaminess when stirred    |
| Basmati            | Long         | India/Pakistan | Light, fluffy, separate | Biryani, pilaf, curries  | Aromatic, elongates further on cooking                   |
| Brown              | Varies       | Global         | Chewy, nutty            | General side dish, bowls | Whole grain, bran intact, higher fiber                   |
| Glutinous (Sticky) | Short        | Southeast Asia | Sticky, clumping        | Mango sticky rice, mochi | Misnomer — gluten-free; high amylopectin makes it sticky |
| Jasmine            | Long         | Thailand       | Soft, slightly sticky   | Southeast Asian dishes   | Floral aroma, softer than basmati                        |
| Sushi (Japonica)   | Short/Medium | Japan          | Sticky, tender          | Sushi, onigiri           | High starch, seasoned with rice vinegar for sushi        |
| Wild Rice          | Long         | North America  | Chewy, nutty            | Pilafs, stuffing, salads | Technically a grass seed, not true rice                  |
