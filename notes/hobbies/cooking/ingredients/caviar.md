## What Is Roe

Roe is the fully ripe egg mass of fish and some other marine animals, such as sea urchin and scallops. It is eaten fresh, cured, or smoked. Caviar is the subset of roe that is salt-cured sturgeon eggs.

## Types

Strictly, caviar is salt-cured roe from sturgeon; roe from other fish is sold as "caviar" with a qualifier. Price follows rarity and egg size, and most types are served cold on a non-metallic spoon so the flavor isn't tainted.

| Type            | Source                        | Description                                                           | Common Uses                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Beluga          | Beluga sturgeon               | Largest, softest eggs; delicate, buttery and mild. The most expensive. | Eaten alone, blinis                         |
| Capelin (Masago) | Capelin                      | Tiny, orange, mildly salty; often dyed.                                | Sushi rolls, garnish                        |
| Kaluga          | Kaluga sturgeon               | Large, firm eggs; rich and buttery, close to beluga.                   | Eaten alone, blinis                         |
| Lumpfish        | Lumpfish                      | Small, hard, very salty black or red eggs; inexpensive.                | Garnish, canapés, dips                      |
| Osetra          | Russian or Danube sturgeon    | Medium eggs; nutty, briny, brown to golden.                            | Eaten alone, blinis, crème fraîche          |
| Paddlefish      | American paddlefish           | Small gray eggs; earthy and mildly briny, a budget sturgeon substitute. | Canapés, deviled eggs                       |
| Salmon (Ikura)  | Salmon                        | Large, bright orange eggs that burst with juicy, salty flavor.         | Sushi, rice bowls, pasta                    |
| Sevruga         | Stellate sturgeon             | Small, gray eggs; the strongest, brinier flavor of the sturgeons.      | Eaten alone, blinis, scrambled eggs         |
| Trout           | Rainbow trout                 | Small, golden eggs; mild and delicate.                                 | Garnish for fish, eggs, canapés             |
| Whitefish       | Lake whitefish                | Tiny golden eggs with a clean, mild flavor.                            | Garnish, deviled eggs, sushi                |
