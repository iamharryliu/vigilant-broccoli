## Chinese

| Soy Sauce     | Color/Body  | Flavor              | Common Uses                        | Notes                                                                |
| ------------- | ----------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dark          | Dark, thick | Sweeter, less salty | Braises, stir-fry color, marinades | Aged longer, often with caramel/molasses added for color and body    |
| Light (Sheng) | Light, thin | Salty, sharp        | Seasoning, dipping, stir-fry       | Saltier and thinner than dark soy, used for flavor rather than color |

## Japanese

| Soy Sauce | Color/Body        | Flavor                | Common Uses                        | Notes                                                                     |
| --------- | ----------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Koikuchi  | Medium-dark       | Balanced, savory      | All-purpose (dipping, cooking)     | The standard Japanese soy sauce, ~80% of Japanese production              |
| Shiro     | Very light, amber | Sweet, mild           | Delicate dishes, chawanmushi       | Mostly wheat with little soy, lightest color and flavor of Japanese types |
| Tamari    | Dark, thick       | Rich, less sharp      | Dipping, gluten-free cooking       | Made with little to no wheat, byproduct of miso production                |
| Usukuchi  | Light, amber      | Saltier than koikuchi | Seasoning without darkening dishes | Lighter color but higher sodium than koikuchi, common in Kansai region    |

## Indonesian

| Soy Sauce   | Color/Body        | Flavor               | Common Uses                | Notes                                                         |
| ----------- | ----------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kecap Manis | Very dark, syrupy | Sweet, molasses-like | Satay, nasi goreng, glazes | Sweetened with palm sugar, much thicker than other soy sauces |

## Other

| Soy Sauce  | Color/Body      | Flavor      | Common Uses                     | Notes                                                           |
| ---------- | --------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Low-Sodium | Similar to base | Milder salt | Everyday cooking, dietary needs | ~40-50% less sodium than regular, often slightly thinner flavor |
