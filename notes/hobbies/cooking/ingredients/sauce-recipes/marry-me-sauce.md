# Marry Me Sauce

## Ingredients

- Oil
- Garlic cloves
- Diced onion
- Vegetable broth
- 1/4 tsp nutritional yeast
- 1/4 tsp pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp oregano
- 1/4 tsp thyme
- Tomato paste
- Sun-dried tomatoes
- Cream
- Parmesan

## Instructions

- Heat oil and pepper flakes in a pan over medium-high heat.
- Sauté diced onion, garlic, and tomato paste until softened and fragrant.
- Add vegetable broth, nutritional yeast, oregano, and thyme. Stir to combine.
- Add sun-dried tomatoes and simmer for a few minutes.
- Stir in cream and let sauce thicken.
