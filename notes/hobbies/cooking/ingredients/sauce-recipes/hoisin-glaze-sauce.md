# Hoisin Glaze Sauce

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- Minced garlic
- Minced ginger
- Soup stock

## Instructions

- Whisk everything together in a bowl.
- Add near the end of cooking and simmer until it reduces to a glaze that coats the food.
