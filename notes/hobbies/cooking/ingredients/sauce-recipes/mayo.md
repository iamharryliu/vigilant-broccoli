# Mayo

## Ingredients

- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice (from 1/2 a lemon)
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1 medium clove garlic, minced
- 1 cup (240 ml) vegetable or canola oil
- Kosher salt

## Instructions

- Place egg, lemon juice, and mustard in the bottom of a jar/cup that just fits the head of an immersion blender (egg/lemon mixture must reach the blades).
- Add garlic, then pour oil on top and let settle for 15 seconds.
- Place immersion blender head at the bottom of the jar, turn on high speed, and hold still (don't pulse or move it) as mayonnaise forms.
- Slowly tilt and lift the blender head until all oil is emulsified.
- Season with salt to taste. Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks.
