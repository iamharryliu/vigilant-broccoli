# Flour

| Flour             | Origin        | Protein Content     | Common Uses                     | Notes                                                                           |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All-Purpose       | USA           | Medium (~10-12%)    | General baking, bread, cookies  | Blend of hard and soft wheat, versatile default flour                           |
| Almond            | Global        | N/A (gluten-free)   | Gluten-free baking, macarons    | Ground blanched almonds, moist crumb, low carb                                  |
| Bread Flour       | USA           | High (~12-14%)      | Yeasted bread, pizza            | Higher gluten for chewy structure and rise                                      |
| Cake Flour        | USA           | Low (~7-9%)         | Cakes, pastries                 | Finely milled, chlorinated, tender/soft crumb                                   |
| Chickpea (Besan)  | India         | N/A (gluten-free)   | Pakora, socca, batters          | Nutty flavor, high protein, common in Indian/Mediterranean cooking              |
| Manitoba Cream    | Canada/Italy  | Very high (~14-16%) | Long-leavened doughs, panettone | Strong gluten, high water absorption, often blended to strengthen weaker flours |
| Quinoa            | South America | N/A (gluten-free)   | Baking, thickening              | Milled from quinoa seeds, earthy flavor, complete protein                       |
| Ragi              | India         | N/A (gluten-free)   | Roti, porridge, dosa            | Milled from finger millet, nutty flavor, high in calcium                        |
| Rice              | Asia          | N/A (gluten-free)   | Noodles, tempura batter         | Neutral flavor, crisp texture when fried                                        |
| Rye               | Europe        | Low (gluten, weak)  | Rye bread, sourdough            | Dense, earthy flavor, low gluten-forming ability                                |
| Semola Rimacinata | Italy         | High (~13%)         | Pasta, bread crust              | Twice-milled durum wheat, coarser than 00, golden color                         |
| Tipo 00           | Italy         | Low-medium (~9-12%) | Pizza, pasta                    | Finely milled, soft wheat, silky texture, gives crisp/tender crust              |
| Whole Wheat       | Global        | Medium-high (~13%)  | Bread, rustic baking            | Includes bran and germ, denser, nuttier flavor                                  |
