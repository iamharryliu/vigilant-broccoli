# Lentils

| Lentil | Color | Form | Cook Time (unsoaked) | Texture When Cooked | Common Uses | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beluga (Black) | Black | Small, round, whole | 20–25 min | Firm, holds shape | Salads, side dishes | Glossy like caviar; keeps shape well |
| Brown (Green-Brown) | Brown to khaki | Medium, whole | 20–30 min | Tender, softens if overcooked | Soups, stews, veggie burgers | Most common supermarket lentil; earthy |
| Castelluccio | Dark green-brown | Tiny, whole | 20–25 min | Firm, delicate skin | Salads, soups, with sausage | Italian (Umbria) IGP; creamy flavor, prized |
| French Green (Puy) | Dark green, mottled | Small, whole | 25–30 min | Firm, peppery | Salads, side dishes | Puy PDO from France; holds shape, mineral flavor |
| Red (Split) | Orange-red | Small, hulled and split | 10–15 min | Soft, breaks down to mush | Dal, purées, soups | Cooks fastest; no soaking; turns yellow-gold when cooked |
| Yellow (Split) | Yellow | Small, hulled and split | 15–20 min | Soft, creamy | Dal, Ethiopian wat, soups | Mild; similar to red, slightly firmer |
