# Vegetarian Ingredient Alternatives

- [Vegan "Chicken" Bouillon Powder](https://veggiedesserts.com/vegan-chicken-broth-seasoning/)
  - 1 cup (65g) nutritional yeast flakes
  - 1½ tbsp onion powder
  - 1 tbsp dried parsley
  - 2 tsp celery salt
  - 1 tsp garlic powder
  - ½ tsp paprika
  - ½ tsp dried thyme
  - ½ tsp dried sage
  - ¼ tsp ground turmeric
  - Blend to a powder, store airtight up to 6 months
  - Use 2 tsp per cup (250ml) boiling water for broth (2 tsp = 1 bouillon cube)
