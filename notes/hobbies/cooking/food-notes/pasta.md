## Basics

- Boil pasta
- Sauce
  - Heat oil on pan.
  - Add garlic to infuse flavour to oil
  - Add aromatics or components to make sauce.
- When pasta is near al dente remove pasta and add to sauce.
- Mix.

### Tips

- Pasta water can be used for making creamy pasta. Boil in less water than the box says — the water ends up far starchier, so it thickens and emulsifies the sauce better.
- Use bronze-cut pasta. The rough, porous surface sheds more starch and gives the sauce something to cling to.
- Toss the pasta and sauce together over the highest heat possible — the hard boil is what forces the starch, water and fat into a proper emulsion.
