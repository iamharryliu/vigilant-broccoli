# Food Explained

Foods that are easy to mistake for a separate ingredient, but are really the same source treated differently.

| Food         | What It Is                                                             | How It's Made                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Taste & Use                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| White Pepper | The same berry as black pepper (_Piper nigrum_), with the skin removed | Berries are picked ripe and soaked in water for ~a week (retting) so the dark outer fruit layer rots and rubs off, leaving the pale inner seed, which is then dried. Black pepper is the unripe berry dried whole, so it keeps that skin as the wrinkled black husk. | Loses the fruity, piney aroma that lives in the skin; keeps the heat (piperine) and gains a fermented, musty, slightly barnyard note. Used where black flecks would look wrong — cream sauces, mashed potatoes, light soups — and heavily in Chinese and Southeast Asian cooking. |
