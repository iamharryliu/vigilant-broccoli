# Brown Butter White Chocolate Matcha Cookies

## Ingredients

- Dry Ingredients
  - 250g all purpose flour
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - ½ tsp salt
  - 2 tbsp matcha powder
- Wet Ingredients
  - 227g unsalted butter
  - 100g granulated sugar
  - 200g light brown sugar
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 2 eggs (room temperature)
- Mix-Ins
  - 175g white chocolate chips

## Instructions

- **Brown the butter**: Heat butter over medium heat until golden yellow and brown specks form, then pour into a heat-proof bowl to cool.
- Combine flour, baking soda, salt, and sifted matcha powder in a medium bowl.
- In a large bowl, mix the browned butter, white sugar, and brown sugar until combined. Add vanilla and eggs, whisk until light and creamy (about 1 minute).
- Gradually mix in the dry ingredients using a spatula until a green dough forms. Fold in the white chocolate chips.
- **Rest the dough**: Chill in the fridge for 1 hour.
- Preheat the oven to 175°C. Prepare two baking sheets with parchment paper.
- Use a 3 tbsp ice cream scoop to portion the cookie dough onto the baking sheets, ensuring cookies are 5-7.5 cm apart.
- Bake for 10-12 minutes or until the bottoms are light brown.
- Cool on the baking sheets for 10 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack for another 15 minutes before serving.
