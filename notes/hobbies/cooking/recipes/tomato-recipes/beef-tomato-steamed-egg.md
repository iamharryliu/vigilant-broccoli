# Beef Tomato Steamed Egg

## Ingredients

- Eggs
  - 4 eggs
- Beef
  - 225g ground beef
- Vegetables
  - 1/4 medium onion, diced
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - 1 Tbsp ginger, minced
  - 1/3 cup bell pepper, diced
  - 8-10 cherry tomatoes, halved
- Sauce
  - 1 Tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 Tbsp dark soy sauce
  - 1 tsp sugar
  - 1 Tbsp oyster sauce
  - 1 tsp rice vinegar
- Seasonings
  - 1 Tbsp chicken powder
  - 1 Tbsp sesame oil
  - 2 Tbsp shaoxing wine
  - 2 tsp corn starch
- Other
  - 1 stalk scallion

## Instructions

- **Whisk together the eggs, chicken powder, sesame oil, and 360ml of warm water. Skim off any bubbles.**
- Fill a large stock pot with 5cm of water and place a steaming rack inside.
- Pour the egg mixture into a serving bowl and place the bowl on the steaming rack. Cover and steam for 10-12 minutes until the egg is a jello-like consistency.
- Remove the steamed egg bowl and set aside.
- Prep the onion, garlic, ginger, bell pepper, and tomato, setting them aside individually.
- Mix the soy sauce, dark soy, sugar, oyster sauce, and rice vinegar and set aside.
- Sear the ground beef in a pan over high heat for 3-5 minutes until cooked through, then set aside.
- In the same pan, add onions and cook for about 2 minutes until they soften. Then add garlic and ginger, cooking for another 2 minutes until fragrant.
- Add bell pepper and cook for another 2 minutes, then add tomatoes and beef. Cook until tomatoes release their juices, about 1-2 minutes.
- Pour in the sauce mixture and stir to combine. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
- Make a slurry by combining corn starch and 60ml of water, then pour it into the mixture and stir until thickened.
- Spoon the beef tomato topping over the steamed egg, top with sliced scallions, and serve immediately.
