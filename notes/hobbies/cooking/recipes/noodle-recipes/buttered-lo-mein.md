# Buttered Lo Mein

## Ingredients

- Noodles
- Greens
- Butter
- Oyster sauce

## Instructions

- Boil the noodles, then add the greens for the last 30 seconds.
- Strain and add the noodles and greens to a pan with the butter and oyster sauce.
