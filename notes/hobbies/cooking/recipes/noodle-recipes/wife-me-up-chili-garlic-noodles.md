# "Wife Me Up" Chili Garlic Noodles

## Ingredients

- Noodles (2 portions)
- Neutral oil (¼ cup)
- Chili flakes (2 tbsp)
- Soy sauce (2 tbsp)
- Black vinegar (1 tbsp)
- Sugar (1 tbsp)
- Garlic (4 cloves), minced
- Ginger (½ in knob), minced
- Green onion (4 stalks), sliced
- Sesame seeds, to taste
- Ground pork (½ lb)

## Instructions

- Cook noodles according to package directions; drain and set aside.
- Brown ground pork in a pan over medium-high heat, breaking it apart, until cooked through.
- Mix soy sauce, black vinegar, and sugar together to make the sauce.
- Heat neutral oil until shimmering, then pour over chili flakes, garlic, and ginger to bloom.
- Toss noodles with the pork, chili oil mixture, and sauce.
- Garnish with green onion and sesame seeds.
