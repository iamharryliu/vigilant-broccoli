# Cha Siu Cauliflower

## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower
- 50 g Chinese cured pork belly, diced

### Marinade

- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 cubes red fermented bean curd
- 1/2 tsp Chinese 5-spice
- 2 tbsp honey/maltose
- 1 tbsp shaoxing wine

## Instructions

### Parcook the cauliflower

- In a large pot, add about 4 cups of water (or enough to nearly submerge the cauliflower), 1/2 cup shaoxing wine, and 2 tbsp salt.
- Bring to a boil, add the cauliflower, cover and simmer 8-10 minutes until tender but not mushy.

### Roast

- Preheat oven to 450 F (230 C).
- Embed the diced cured pork belly into the cauliflower.
- Combine the garlic, light soy, dark soy, white pepper, hoisin, red fermented bean curd, 5-spice, honey/maltose, and shaoxing wine into a paste.
- Place the cauliflower on a roasting tray and brush over the marinade. Pour a few tbsp of water in the tray to prevent the marinade from burning.
- Roast 20-30 minutes, brushing on more marinade every 10 minutes until fully cooked throughout and charred.
