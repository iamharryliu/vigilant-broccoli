# Caramelized Vegetable Lentil Stew

## Ingredients

- carrots
- potatoes
- onions
- garlic
- chillies
- peppers
- tomato paste
- flour
- butter
- wine/water
- stock
- lentils
- pepper
- smoked paprika
- mushroom soy sauce

## Instructions

- sear carrots
- bake potatoes
- caramelize onions, garlic, chillies, peppers and deglaze
- add carrots
- add tomato paste
- add flour and butter
- brown
- deglaze with wine and stock
- add lentils
- add potatoes
- add pepper and smoked paprika
- add mushroom soy sauce and salt to taste
