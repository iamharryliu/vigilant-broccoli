# Chinese Mung Bean Soup with Lily Bulb

Makes about 4–5 servings

## Ingredients

- 120 g dried mung beans
- 1–1.2 L water
- 20 g dried lily bulbs, rinsed
- 40 g rock sugar

## Instructions

- Rinse the mung beans thoroughly. Add them to a pot with cold water, bring to a boil, then drain. This first blanch helps remove any earthy taste.
- Return the beans to the pot and add 1–1.2 L fresh cold water.
- Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to low heat. Cover partially and simmer for about 1½ hours, or until the beans have broken down and the soup becomes thick and slightly sandy in texture.
- Add the rinsed dried lily petals and rock sugar.
- Simmer for another 30 minutes, until the lily bulbs are tender and the sugar has dissolved.
- Serve warm, or chill completely in the fridge before serving.
