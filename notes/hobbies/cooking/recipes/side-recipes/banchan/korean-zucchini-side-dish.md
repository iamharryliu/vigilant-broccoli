# Korean Zucchini Side Dish (Hobak Namul)

## Ingredients

- 1 zucchini
- 1 green onion, sliced finely
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp Korean red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp salt

## Instructions

- Cut zucchini into thin discs, salt with ½ tsp salt, let sit 10 minutes, then pat dry and squeeze out excess water.
- Heat sesame oil over medium-high heat, stir-fry zucchini ~5 minutes until softened and lightly golden.
- Add garlic, green onion, fish sauce, sugar, and red pepper flakes, stir-fry 1 more minute.
- Cool and serve at room temperature or chilled.
