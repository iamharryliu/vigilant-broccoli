# Crushed Cucumber Salad

## Table of Contents

- [Ingredients](#ingredients)
- [Instructions](#instructions)

## Ingredients

For about 2 servings:

- 2 medium cucumbers (about 400 g)
- 1/2 tsp salt, for drawing out water
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1–2 tsp chili oil, to taste
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds (optional)
- 1 tbsp chopped cilantro (optional)

## Instructions

- Wash the cucumbers and trim the ends. On a stable chopping board, gently crush them with the flat of a broad knife or a rolling pin until they split, then cut into bite-size pieces. The rough surfaces help hold the dressing.
- Toss with the salt and leave in a colander over a bowl for 10–15 minutes. Drain well and pat away excess moisture so the dressing stays flavorful.
- Mix the garlic, soy sauce, rice vinegar, chili oil, sesame oil and sugar until the sugar dissolves. Start with 1 tsp chili oil and add more to taste.
- Toss the drained cucumbers with the dressing. Taste before adding any extra salt, since the cucumbers and soy sauce are already salted.
- Add the sesame seeds and cilantro, if using. Serve promptly, or chill for 10 minutes and serve while crisp.
