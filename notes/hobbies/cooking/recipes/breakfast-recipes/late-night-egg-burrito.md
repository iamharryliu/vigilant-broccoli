# Late Night Egg Burrito

Inspired by the bean and egg burrito from Padma Lakshmi's _Taste the Nation_.

## Ingredients

- Refried beans
- Egg
- Cheese
- Salsa verde
- Salt, to taste
- Tortilla

## Instructions

- Mix together refried beans, egg, cheese, and salsa verde.
- Cook the mixture and season with salt to taste.
- Wrap in a tortilla.
