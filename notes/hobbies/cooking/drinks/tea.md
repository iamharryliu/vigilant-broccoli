## Categories

| Category | Oxidation           | Brewing temperature (°C) | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Black    | Full                | 95–100                   | Fully oxidized; strongest flavor, base for chai and many blends                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Green    | Minimal             | 70–80                    | Unoxidized, pan-fired or steamed to halt oxidation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Herbal   | None (not true tea) | 100                      | Infusions of herbs/flowers/fruit, no Camellia sinensis leaf                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Oolong   | Partial             | 85–100                   | Ranges from light/floral to dark/roasted depending on oxidation level. Oolong and green tea are distinct tea categories: green tea is heated early to halt oxidation, while oolong is partially oxidized before heating. Lightly oxidized oolong can look green and taste fresh or floral, but it is still oolong. |
| Pu-erh   | Fermented           | 95–100                   | Aged/microbially fermented, sold as raw (sheng) or ripe (shou)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Scented  | Depends on base tea | Follow the base tea      | Tea scented with flowers, such as jasmine; can also belong to a processing category such as green, white, or oolong                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| White    | Minimal             | 75–85                    | Least processed; young leaves/buds, delicate flavor                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Yellow   | Light               | 75–80                    | Similar to green but with an extra slow-drying/"sealing" step                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Terminology

| Term         | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fermentation | Microbial transformation of leaves over time (distinct from oxidation), used for pu-erh                                                                                                                |
| Oxidation    | Enzymatic browning of tea leaves on exposure to air after picking/rolling, which darkens color and deepens flavor; halting it early (heat/steam) keeps tea green, letting it run fully makes black tea |
