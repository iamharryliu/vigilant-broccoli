# Tea

| Type              | Oxidation                    | Notes                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Herbal            | None (not true tea)          | Infusions of herbs/flowers/fruit, no Camellia sinensis leaf                         |
| White             | Minimal                      | Least processed; young leaves/buds, delicate flavor                                 |
| Green             | Minimal                      | Unoxidized, pan-fired or steamed to halt oxidation                                  |
| Matcha            | Minimal                      | Shade-grown green tea, stone-ground into powder, whisked rather than steeped        |
| Jasmine           | Minimal (usually green base) | Scented tea — green (sometimes white/oolong) leaves layered with jasmine blossoms   |
| Yellow            | Light                        | Similar to green but with an extra slow-drying/"sealing" step                       |
| Oolong            | Partial                      | Ranges from light/floral to dark/roasted depending on oxidation level               |
| Black             | Full                         | Fully oxidized; strongest flavor, base for chai and many blends                     |
| Chai              | Full (black base)            | Black tea brewed/simmered with milk and spices (cardamom, cinnamon, ginger, cloves) |
| Earl Grey         | Full                         | Black tea blend flavored with bergamot orange oil                                   |
| English Breakfast | Full                         | Black tea blend (often Assam/Ceylon/Kenyan), malty and robust                       |
| Pu-erh            | Fermented                    | Aged/microbially fermented, sold as raw (sheng) or ripe (shou)                      |

## Terminology

| Term         | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fermentation | Microbial transformation of leaves over time (distinct from oxidation), used for pu-erh                                                                                                                |
| Oxidation    | Enzymatic browning of tea leaves on exposure to air after picking/rolling, which darkens color and deepens flavor; halting it early (heat/steam) keeps tea green, letting it run fully makes black tea |
