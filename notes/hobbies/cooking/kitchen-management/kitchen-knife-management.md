# Kitchen Knife Management

## Steel

- Stainless Steel
  - Resists rust and staining, low maintenance.
  - Harder to get as sharp, dulls faster than carbon steel.
  - Good default for most home kitchens.
- Carbon Steel
  - Takes and holds a sharper edge, easier to resharpen.
  - Reacts with acidic foods and rusts if left wet — dry immediately after washing.
  - Preferred by many professional and Japanese-style knives.

## Western vs Japanese

- Western (e.g. German knives)
  - Softer steel, thicker/heavier blade, edge angle ~20° per side.
  - More forgiving, better for rocking cuts and heavier tasks (bones, joints).
- Japanese
  - Harder steel, thinner/lighter blade, edge angle ~10-15° per side.
  - Sharper and more precise, more brittle — avoid twisting or cutting bone.

## Types of Knives

- [Knife Parts](https://ca.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=knife%20parts)
- [Types of Knives](https://ca.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=types%20of%20kitchen%20knives)
- Chef's Knife — all-purpose, most used knife.
- Santoku — Japanese all-purpose, flatter edge, often with granton (dimpled) sides.
- Paring Knife — small, precision work (peeling, trimming).
- Bread Knife — serrated, for crusty or soft baked goods.
- Utility Knife — mid-size, between chef's and paring knife.
- Boning/Fillet Knife — thin, flexible, for meat and fish.
- Cleaver — heavy, for chopping through bone.
- Nakiri — Japanese, rectangular blade for vegetables.

## Sharpening & Maintenance

- Honing rod
  - Realigns the edge between uses, doesn't remove material.
  - Use regularly, every few uses or before each use.
  - [How to use a honing steel](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+use+a+honing+steel)
- Sharpening (whetstone/sharpening block)
  - Removes material to form a new edge, use when honing no longer restores sharpness.
  - [Whetstone](https://www.google.com/search?q=whetstone)
  - [How to use a whetstone](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+use+a+whetstone)
- Routine: hone regularly during/between use, sharpen with a whetstone only when the knife stays dull after honing.
