## Stovetop

| Material | Heat Conduction | Heat Retention | Best Use Cases | Avoid For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aluminum | Very high | Low | Fast boiling, pasta water, sautéing | Acidic sauces (reacts, can taste metallic) |
| Anodized aluminum | High | Low | Everyday sautéing, eggs, simmering sauces | Dishwasher, metal utensils |
| Carbon steel | High | Medium | Stir-fry (wok), searing, crepes, fried eggs once seasoned | Long acidic simmers, soaking |
| Cast iron | Low, uneven | Very high | Searing steak, frying, blackened dishes | Delicate fish, long acidic simmers when bare |
| Ceramic nonstick | Medium | Low | Low to medium heat eggs and pancakes | High heat, metal utensils, long-term use (coating wears) |
| Copper | Very high | Low | Precise sauces, candy, custards | Induction (unless clad with a magnetic layer), heavy daily use |
| Enameled cast iron | Low, uneven | Very high | Braises, stews, soups, no-knead bread in a pot | Dry high-heat preheating, chipping from impacts |
| Nonstick (PTFE) | Medium | Low | Eggs, pancakes, delicate fish | High-heat searing, broiling, metal utensils |
| Stainless steel (clad) | Medium to high | Medium | Searing, pan sauces (fond), boiling, general use | Eggs without enough fat, sticky foods at low heat |
| Tempered glass | Low | Medium | Lids for watching simmers and steaming only | Direct burner heat for pots, sudden temperature changes |

## Oven Baking

| Material | Heat Conduction | Heat Retention | Best Use Cases | Avoid For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aluminum (bare) | Very high | Low | Cookies, sheet-pan vegetables, cakes, even browning | Acidic foods (stains and reacts) |
| Cast iron | Low, uneven | Very high | Cornbread, deep-dish pizza, roasts, crusty bread | Delicate cakes, quick-cooling needs |
| Enameled cast iron | Low, uneven | Very high | Dutch oven bread, braises, casseroles | Broiler or heat above the lid knob's rating |
| Foil | High | Very low | Covering to prevent over-browning, lining pans, packets | Acidic foods in direct contact, microwave |
| Glass (tempered) | Low | High | Casseroles, lasagna, roasted fruit, baked pasta | Broiler, stovetop, moving from fridge or freezer to hot oven, delicate crisping |
| Nonstick (dark coating) | High | Low | Muffins, loaf cakes, easy release | High heat above the coating's limit, metal utensils |
| Parchment | N/A (liner) | N/A | Lining sheets and cake pans, en papillote | Broiler, temperatures above about 220°C / 425°F |
| Silicone | Very low | Low | Muffins, molded desserts, nonstick liners | Crisping and browning |
| Stainless steel | Medium | Medium | Roasting pans, sheet pans for roasting meat and vegetables | Delicate cakes (can brown unevenly) |
| Stoneware | Low | High | Pizza, bread, biscuits, even baking | Sudden temperature changes, soap-soaked cleaning |
