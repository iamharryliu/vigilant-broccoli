## 12 Bar Blues Progressions

### Standard

```
| I  | I  | I  | I  |
| IV | IV | I  | I  |
| V  | IV | I  | I  |
```

### Slow Change

```
| I  | I  | I  | I  |
| IV | IV | I  | I  |
| V  | IV | I  | V  |
```

### Quick Change

```
| I  | IV | I  | I  |
| IV | IV | I  | I  |
| V  | IV | I  | V  |
```
