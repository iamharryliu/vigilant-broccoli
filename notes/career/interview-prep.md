## Tell Me About Yourself

| Theme | What to Present | Talking Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collaboration | You work well with engineers, designers and stakeholders. | Pairing, code reviews, clear communication and early alignment. |
| Culture | You build community and share knowledge. | Mentoring, onboarding newcomers, documenting, demos and internal talks. |
| Delivery | You take work from idea to production and keep it running. | Shipping end to end, owning deployment and operating what you build. |
| Growth | You learn quickly and keep widening your range. | Picking up new tools, working across the stack and acting on feedback. |
| Impact | Your work saves time and moves a business metric. | Automating manual work, improving performance and tying work to outcomes. |
| Ownership | You take responsibility for outcomes, not only tasks. | Driving requirements, making decisions and following through after release. |
| Reliability | You build things that stay up and can be recovered. | Monitoring, alerting, backups, testing and calm incident handling. |

## Questions

| Topic | Questions | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hiring Process | • What does the hiring process look like?<br>• What is the expected hiring date?<br>• Why is role open? Are we replacing someone, or is it a new role on the team? | • Learn how the hiring process runs, when it ends and what happens next. |
| Logistics | • Is the role remote, hybrid or in office?<br>• How many days a week are expected in office, and which days?<br>• Is the in-office policy likely to change, and has it changed recently?<br>• Is there flexibility on location, such as working remotely for a stretch of time?<br>• Are core hours expected, or can I set my own schedule? | • A schedule, working hours, workload and expectations that fit my lifestyle. |
| Compensation | • What is the salary range?<br>• What benefits are provided? What amenities are there?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ provided devices (machine/phone)?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ coding assistant provider?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ budget for self development? | • Reasonable compensation. |
| Responsibilities | • What would the day to day look like for my role? What does it look like for you?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ How much time do you have available to develop versus being in meetings?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ Which meetings are mandatory, such as team syncs, one-on-ones with the manager or all-hands, and how often?<br>• What's the balance between firefighting and project work in an average week?<br>• How much ownership do I have over technical decisions? | • Understand what I am responsible for. |
| Product | • What do they like and dislike about it?<br>• What's the most interesting thing that they have worked on or learned on the project?<br>• Is the project greenfield or brownfield? | • A reliable, useful product I would be proud to work on. |
| Environment and Team | • What are the company values?<br>• What is the team I would be joining like, and how do they work together?<br>• What is the team structure?<br>• What are the biggest challenges that the team is facing?<br>• How is the onboarding process for a new hire?<br>• How would you describe the culture and how is it maintained if there is one?<br>• What do you like most about working here?<br>• What do you wish you could improve? | • A collaborative and supportive team, with people I can learn from and work well with. |
| Management | • Who would be the direct manager?<br>• How would you describe your management style?<br>• How do you give feedback? | • Being able to manage myself or having a good manager:<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ Trusted to own my work and decisions without micromanagement.<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ Or a manager who sets clear expectations, provides support and communicates openly, giving clear direction I can execute on. |
| Performance | • What does being successful look like in the first 90 days<br>• How is performance measured and how often is feedback given?<br>• What distinguishes a senior engineer on this team? | • Learn what success looks like and how it is measured. |
| Practices | • Current stack and why it was chosen?<br>• What is the CI/CD approach?<br>• What does the code review process look like?<br>• What does the testing process look like?<br>• How are environments and deployments handled?<br>• How are scalability and performance handled?<br>• What are the documentation processes?<br>• Every company is carrying some amount of _technical debt_, what's the tech debt situation here?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ How much they think there is?<br>&emsp;&emsp;◦ What they're doing about it? | • Well structured workspace with clear processes. |
| Company Direction | • How does leadership prioritize long-term technical investments vs. short-term business needs?<br>• Where do they see the team in a year?<br>• What are the biggest risks or unknowns the company is facing in the near future? | • Confidence in the company and leadership. |
| Opportunities | • How does the company support professional development?<br>• Is there an active mentoring strategy? | • Opportunities to develop in my career and have impact, not just tech-wise. |
| Reflection | • Is there anything about my background that gives you hesitation or that I can clarify? | • Fill in any gaps the interviewer may have about you. |
