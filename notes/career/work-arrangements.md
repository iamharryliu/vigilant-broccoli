## Comparison

| Arrangement | Relationship | Pay | Benefits and Protections | Taxes | Typical Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contractor | Engaged by a client, often through an agency, to fill a role or deliver a project. | Hourly or daily rate. | None from the client; some through an agency or umbrella company. | Handled by you, or by the agency or umbrella company. | Months, often renewed. |
| Fixed-term employee | Employee of the company until a set end date. | Salary. | Same as full-time while it lasts. | Withheld by the employer. | Months to a few years. |
| Freelancer | Self-employed, selling discrete pieces of work to many clients. | Per project, deliverable or hour. | None; you buy your own insurance and pension. | Handled by you. | Days to weeks per job. |
| Full-time employee | Employee with no end date. | Salary. | Paid leave, pension, sick pay, notice period and employment law protection. | Withheld by the employer. | Open-ended. |
| Independent consultant | Self-employed, selling expertise and advice to a few clients. | Daily rate, retainer or fixed fee per engagement. | None; you buy your own insurance and pension. | Handled by you. | Weeks to months per engagement. |
| Part-time employee | Employee working fewer hours than the standard week. | Pro-rata salary or hourly wage. | Same as full-time, scaled to hours. | Withheld by the employer. | Open-ended or fixed-term. |

## Contract vs Freelance vs Consultant

The terms overlap and are often used interchangeably; the differences are in what is sold and how the client directs the work.

| Aspect | Consultant | Contractor | Freelancer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client count at once | One to a few. | Usually one. | Several. |
| Direction | Advises; the client decides and often implements. | Works inside the client's team, close to an employee. | Delivers to a brief, working how and when they choose. |
| Pricing | Value, daily rate or retainer. | Hourly or daily rate. | Per project or hourly. |
| What is sold | Expertise, diagnosis and recommendations. | Capacity: a person filling a role. | A deliverable. |

A contractor who works fixed hours, under a manager, for one client over a long period can be treated as an employee by tax authorities (misclassification), which shifts tax and liability onto the client. Clients often avoid this by limiting contract length or requiring an agency.

## Independent Consultant

An independent consultant runs their own business rather than being hired out by a consultancy. They find their own clients, set their own rates and carry the risk of gaps between engagements.

### Setting Up

| Step | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Business entity | Choose between a sole trader (simple, personal liability) and a limited company (more admin, limited liability, more tax planning). An umbrella company is an option for trying it without registering. |
| Tax registration | Register for business tax and, above the local threshold, VAT. |
| Bank account | Keep business money separate from personal money. |
| Insurance | Professional liability (errors and omissions) and, for a company, general liability. Some clients require proof. |
| Contract template | Scope, deliverables, rate, payment terms, intellectual property ownership, confidentiality, liability cap and termination notice. |
| Invoicing and bookkeeping | Use accounting software or an accountant; set aside tax from every invoice. |
| Pension and buffer | No employer pays into a pension; save several months of expenses to cover gaps. |

### Pricing

Start from the income you need, not from a salary:

```text
rate = (target income + business costs + pension + insurance) / billable hours
```

- Working hours in a year are roughly 1,700–1,800 after holidays.
- Billable hours are lower: selling, admin, learning and gaps leave about 50–75% utilization.
- A daily rate is typically 1.5–2× an equivalent employee's daily salary cost to cover these gaps and the missing benefits.
- Fixed-fee and value-based pricing reward finishing fast; hourly pricing rewards time spent.

### Finding Clients

- Former employers and colleagues are the most common first clients.
- Agencies and brokers place consultants for a margin on the rate.
- Freelance marketplaces suit smaller, shorter work.
- Visible expertise through writing, talks or open source brings inbound work over time.

### Pros and Cons

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| Choice of clients, projects and schedule. | Income stops between engagements. |
| Higher rates than equivalent salaries. | No paid leave, sick pay, pension contributions or employment protection. |
| Exposure to many companies and problems. | Time spent on sales, admin, accounting and taxes. |
| Tax deductions for business expenses. | Less career progression, mentorship and team belonging. |

## Choosing an Arrangement

| If You Want | Consider |
| --- | --- |
| A trial of independent work with little admin | Contracting through an agency or umbrella company. |
| Higher pay and are fine with gaps | Contracting or independent consulting. |
| Many short, varied projects | Freelancing. |
| Stability, benefits and progression | Full-time employment. |
| Time for other commitments with job security | Part-time employment. |
| To sell expertise rather than hours | Independent consulting. |

## Sweden

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Aktiebolag (AB) | Limited company; requires share capital and annual reports, and lets profit be paid as salary or dividends. |
| Egenanställning | Umbrella company: you find clients, the company invoices them and pays you a salary minus a fee. No F-tax needed. |
| Egenavgifter | Social contributions a sole trader pays on profit, the self-employed equivalent of employer contributions. |
| Enskild firma | Sole trader; quick to register with Bolagsverket and Skatteverket, with personal liability for debts. |
| F-skatt | F-tax approval from Skatteverket; lets you invoice clients and pay your own tax and contributions. |
| FA-skatt | Approval for both employment (A-tax) and business (F-tax) income, for working on the side of a job. |
| Konsultmäklare | Consultant broker that places independent consultants with clients for a margin. |
| Provanställning | Probation period, at most six months, before a permanent contract. |
| Särskild visstidsanställning | Fixed-term employment; becomes permanent after more than twelve months with one employer within five years. |
| Tillsvidareanställning | Permanent employment, the default contract type. |
