# Sony WH-1000XM4 Gestures

| Action | Gesture | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Answer / end call | Double-tap the right-earcup touch sensor. | Requires a Bluetooth phone connection supporting HFP/HSP. Sony documents ordinary phone calls; calling apps are not supported. |
| Next track | Swipe forward horizontally and release. | Bluetooth playback controls require AVRCP; support varies by device and app. |
| Play / pause | Double-tap. | Bluetooth playback only; device and app support varies. |
| Previous track | Swipe backward horizontally and release. | May restart the current track during playback; Bluetooth device and app support varies. |
| Quick Attention | Cover the entire right-earcup touch sensor with your palm and keep it there. | Temporarily lowers music and lets ambient sound through; ends when you lift your hand. Does not toggle Speak-to-Chat. Unavailable during phone conversations. |
| Speak-to-Chat on / off | Hold two fingers on the right-earcup touch sensor until the voice confirmation. | No app needed. To disable, wait for “Speak-to-chat deactivated”; repeat to enable. If it fails, slightly separate your fingers and lay their pads flat directly on the sensor. When enabled, speaking automatically pauses or mutes music and lets conversation through. |
| Touch controls | Use bare finger pads on the flat touch sensor on the right earcup while wearing the headphones. | All gestures here use this sensor. With the supplied audio cable and power on, Quick Attention and Speak-to-Chat work, but playback and volume gestures do not. |
| Voice assistant | Hold one finger on the sensor for about 2 seconds. | For enabled Siri on a Bluetooth-connected iPhone, or the configured Google app on a compatible Android phone, while idle or playing music. Phone/app support varies; the Google app gesture is unavailable if CUSTOM is assigned to Google Assistant or Alexa. |
| Volume down | Swipe down; swipe down and hold for continuous adjustment. | Release at the desired level. Call volume adjusts only during a conversation and is separate from music volume. |
| Volume up | Swipe up; swipe up and hold for continuous adjustment. | Release at the desired level. Bluetooth device and app support varies. |
