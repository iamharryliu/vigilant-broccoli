## Wall Plugs

| Type | Image | Description | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type A | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/NEMA-1-15P-plugs-old-new-electric-toy.jpg?width=200" alt="Type A plugs" width="200"> | Two flat parallel prongs, ungrounded. | North America, Japan |
| Type B | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/NEMA-AC-Power-Plugs.jpg?width=200" alt="Type A (left) and Type B (right) plugs" width="200"> | Two flat prongs plus a round grounding pin. Image shows Type A on the left and Type B on the right. | North America |
| Type C (Europlug) | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Type_C_Europlug.jpg?width=200" alt="Type C Europlug" width="200"> | Two round pins, ungrounded. Fits Type E and F sockets. | Europe, much of Africa, Asia and South America |
| Type D |  | Three round pins in a triangle, grounded. | India, Nepal, Namibia |
| Type E/F (Schuko) | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Schuko_plug_and_socket_annotated.png?width=200" alt="Schuko plug and socket" width="200"> | Two round prongs with side earthing clips, grounded. | Most of Europe |
| Type G | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/G_type_plug_and_socket.png?width=200" alt="Type G plug and socket" width="200"> | Three rectangular blades, grounded, with a fuse in the plug. | UK, Ireland, Malta, Cyprus, Singapore, Hong Kong |
| Type I | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Aust_AC_plug.jpg?width=200" alt="Type I plug" width="200"> | Two flat blades in a V shape, plus a vertical grounding blade on the grounded version. | Australia, New Zealand, China, Argentina |
| Type J | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/AC_Power_Plug_Type_J.jpg?width=200" alt="Type J plug" width="200"> | Three round pins, grounded, with the earth pin offset. | Switzerland, Liechtenstein |
| Type K | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Danish_type_K_plug.jpg?width=200" alt="Type K plug" width="200"> | Three round pins, grounded. | Denmark, Greenland |
| Type L | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Ita_plug_16A_10A.jpg?width=200" alt="Type L plugs" width="200"> | Three round pins in a line, grounded, in 10 A and 16 A sizes. | Italy, Chile |
| Type N | <img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/NBR_14136_plugs_and_outlet.jpg?width=200" alt="Type N plugs and outlet" width="200"> | Three round pins, grounded, in 10 A and 20 A sizes. | Brazil |
