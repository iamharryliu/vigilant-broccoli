# Light EDC

A minimal everyday carry that fits in a jacket pocket or the front pocket of a small bag — the things worth having on any outing without committing to a full [Travel Packlist](./packlist.md).

## Table of Contents

- [Carry](#carry)
- [Items](#items)

## Carry

- Keep it in one small pouch so it moves between bags as a single unit.
- Restock after each trip — these are all consumables.

## Items

- Personal Care Items
  - Chapstick
  - Hand sanitizer
  - Hand wipes
  - Contact lenses — an extra pair, if you wear contacts
  - Floss wand
  - Mints/gum
  - Moisturizer
  - Näsdukar (tissues)
  - First Aid
    - Alcohol wipes
    - Bandaids
