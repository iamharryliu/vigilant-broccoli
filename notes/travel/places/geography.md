## Regions

| Term                    | Description                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Balkans**             | Southeastern Europe peninsula bounded by the Adriatic, Ionian, Aegean, and Black Seas; includes Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, Kosovo, Bulgaria, and the European part of Turkey. |
| **Baltics**             | The three states on the eastern Baltic Sea coast: Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania.                                               |
| **Benelux**             | Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg, grouped for their historical economic union.                                          |
| **Caucasus**            | Region between the Black and Caspian Seas spanning Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, and parts of southern Russia.                  |
| **Iberia**              | The peninsula in southwestern Europe occupied by Spain and Portugal.                                                            |
| **Levant**              | Eastern Mediterranean region including Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Israel, Palestine, and parts of southern Turkey.                 |
| **Maghreb**             | Northwest Africa comprising Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, and Libya.                                                               |
| **Nordics**             | Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Iceland, grouped by shared cultural and political ties.                                  |
| **Scandinavia**         | Denmark, Norway, and Sweden; sometimes used loosely to mean the Nordics.                                                        |
