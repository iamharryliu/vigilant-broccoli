# Geography

- [Regions](#regions)

## Regions

| Term                  | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Anatolia**          | The peninsula making up the Asian portion of Turkey, bounded by the Black, Aegean, and Mediterranean Seas.                                                                                                                                  |
| **Arabian Peninsula** | Landmass in southwest Asia comprising Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Oman, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, and Kuwait.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Balkans**           | Southeastern Europe peninsula bounded by the Adriatic, Ionian, Aegean, and Black Seas; includes Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, Kosovo, Bulgaria, and the European part of Turkey. |
| **Baltics**           | The three states on the eastern Baltic Sea coast: Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Benelux**           | Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg, grouped for their historical economic union.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Caucasus**          | Region between the Black and Caspian Seas spanning Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, and parts of southern Russia.                                                                                                                              |
| **Fertile Crescent**  | Crescent-shaped region of the Levant and Mesopotamia (Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Israel, Palestine, and southeastern Turkey) noted as the birthplace of early agriculture and civilization.                                              |
| **Iberia**            | The peninsula in southwestern Europe occupied by Spain and Portugal.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Kurdistan**         | Cultural region of the Kurds spanning parts of Turkey, Iran, Iraq, and Syria; not a sovereign state, though the Kurdistan Region is an autonomous federal territory within Iraq.                                                            |
| **Levant**            | Eastern Mediterranean region including Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Israel, Palestine, and parts of southern Turkey.                                                                                                                             |
| **Maghreb**           | Northwest Africa comprising Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, and Libya.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Mashriq**           | The Arabic-speaking Middle East east of the Maghreb, covering Egypt, the Levant, Iraq, and the Arabian Peninsula.                                                                                                                           |
| **MENA**              | Middle East and North Africa; shorthand grouping the Arabian Peninsula, Levant, Mesopotamia, Iran, and the Maghreb.                                                                                                                         |
| **Middle East**       | Region spanning southwest Asia and Egypt, including the Levant, Mesopotamia (Iraq), the Arabian Peninsula, Iran, and Turkey.                                                                                                                |
| **Nordics**           | Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Iceland, grouped by shared cultural and political ties.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Scandinavia**       | Denmark, Norway, and Sweden; sometimes used loosely to mean the Nordics.                                                                                                                                                                    |
