## Food & Drink

### Coffee

| Cafe                                                | Description                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [April Coffee](https://aprilcoffeeroasters.com/)    | Roaster and cafe in Nørrebro, known for light roasts.               |
| [Andersen & Maillard](https://andersenmaillard.dk/) | Roastery and bakery hybrid; cruffins and filter coffee.             |
| [Coffee Collective](https://coffeecollective.dk/)   | Direct-trade pioneer with bars at Torvehallerne and Jægersborggade. |
| [La Cabra](https://lacabra.dk/)                     | Aarhus roaster with a bakery and cafe in the city centre.           |
| [Prolog Coffee](https://prologcoffeebar.com/)       | Specialty bar in the Kødbyen meatpacking district.                  |
| [Hart Bageri](https://hartbageri.com/)              | Bakery in Frederiksberg serving Coffee Collective beans.            |

## Entertainment

| Venue                                                 | Description                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Tivoli Gardens](https://www.tivoli.dk/en)            | Historic amusement park and gardens next to Central Station.                            |
| [Bakken](https://www.bakken.dk/)                      | The world's oldest amusement park, in Klampenborg; free entry, pay per ride.            |
| [Liseberg](https://www.liseberg.com/)                 | Sweden's largest amusement park, in Gothenburg — reachable as a long day trip by train. |
| [Den Blå Planet](https://denblaaplanet.dk/en/)        | Northern Europe's largest aquarium, near the airport.                                   |
| [Experimentarium](https://www.experimentarium.dk/en/) | Hands-on science centre in Hellerup.                                                    |
| [Copenhagen Zoo](https://www.zoo.dk/en)               | Zoo in Frederiksberg.                                                                   |
| [Royal Arena](https://www.royalarena.dk/)             | Main indoor arena for concerts and sport.                                               |
| [Det Kongelige Teater](https://kglteater.dk/)         | Royal Danish Theatre — opera, ballet, and drama.                                        |
| [CopenHill](https://www.copenhill.dk/en)              | Year-round dry ski slope and climbing wall on top of a waste-to-energy plant.           |
| [Nordisk Film Biografer](https://www.nfbio.dk/)       | Main cinema chain, including the Imperial.                                              |
